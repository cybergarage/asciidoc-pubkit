## English heading criteria

Compare each title with the section body, its parent and sibling titles. Name
the supported subject, operation or comparison without inventing benefits or
omitting conditions. Task headings and conceptual headings can coexist. Follow
the book's capitalization and spelling preferences; do not impose imperative
forms, sentence case or a universal length limit. Exact duplicate sibling titles
are review candidates, not errors. Preserve justified repeated titles.

Distinguish comparison subjects from actions that identify or distinguish them.
Use body evidence to decide whether a title names a conceptual comparison, a
procedure or an observation limit. Keep clear technical terms and necessary
qualifications. Record original title, actual alternative, evidence and decision
for every selected heading, including those without candidates.

Preserve section hierarchy, order, IDs and protected inline material. Insert
only an exact saved permitted_id_anchor when needed to retain a generated ID.
Body text is read-only evidence; missing body evidence remains needs-evidence.
