## Whole-outline assessment

Read the complete outline for the intended readers, prerequisites and purpose.
Compare chapter order, parent/child scope, sibling distinctions, granularity,
terminology and transitions against the supplied body evidence. A plausible
outline is not proof that the body supports each title. Mixed task and conceptual
headings are allowed; follow the book's conventions rather than one template.

Record each selected title's ID, original, proposed alternative, reason and
keep/revise/needs-evidence decision. Deeper or unselected headings remain context
only. Missing tables, figures or implementation evidence must be reported.
Chapter moves, merges, splits and hierarchy changes are separate proposals;
outline diagnosis never expands mapped title edit permissions. Preserve IDs.
Mechanical verification does not establish semantic or publication readiness.
